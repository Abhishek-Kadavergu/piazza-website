Analytics vs Reporting: Unlocking Data's Full Potential

Analytics involves data interpreting where reporting involving presenting factual, accurate data. Analytics answers why something is happening based on data, whereas reporting tells what's happening. Analytics delivers recommendations, but reporting is more about organizing and summarizing data.
