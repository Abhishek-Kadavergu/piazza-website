Our Journey

Building the Future,
One Milestone at a Time

From a bold idea in 2020 to a global technology partner — here's how we've grown, evolved, and delivered impact at every step.

2020

PCG was born with a mission to transform businesses through technology, AI, and strategic consulting — starting from a single room with a bold vision.

2021

Reached our first major milestone — trusted by 50+ clients across mortgage, fintech, and SaaS industries, building real-world impact.

2022

Launched cybersecurity, engineering consulting, and product design divisions — expanding our capabilities to serve broader markets.

2023

Pioneered Conversational AI, Intelligent OCR, and Document AI solutions — transforming enterprise automation for our clients.

2024

Expanded internationally to serve clients across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific with dedicated regional teams.

2025

Released our flagship AI-powered mortgage automation platform and internal SaaS products, marking a new era of product innovation.

2026

Scaling globally with new offices, strategic partnerships, and next-gen AI-driven consulting services — the journey continues.
